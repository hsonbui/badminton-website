Badminton Court Boundaries & Service Rules

Toggle between Singles and Doubles to see the in-bounds court areas and service zones highlighted.

Singles Play Rules

In singles, the court is long and narrow. The side alleys are "OUT" for both rally play and serving. The back boundary line is the furthest line for both serving and general play.

  • Serve: Must land past the short service line and inside the center & inner sideline.
  • Score Even: Server stands on the right service court.
  • Score Odd: Server stands on the left service court.

Techniques

The 5 Fundamental Badminton Shots

Master these core strokes to build an unpredictable attacking and defensive repertoire.

Offensive

The Forehand Smash

The primary attacking weapon hit with steep downward trajectory and maximum power.

Target: Midcourt open floor / Body
Tactical

The Drop Shot

A delicate deception stroke disguised as a clear/smash that dips just over the net.

Target: Opponent front court corners
Defensive / Baseline

The Clear (Lob)

A deep high shot pushing the opponent back to their rear boundary to reset tempo.

Target: Rear boundary baseline
Aggressive

The Net Kill

A quick wrist flick at the tape against a weak tumbling return from the opponent.

Target: Steep downward into forecourt
Speed Rally

The Drive

A flat, aggressive horizontal shot skimming the net, vital for fast doubles exchanges.

Target: Opponent body or sidelines
Offensive Stroke

Forehand Smash Execution

Position yourself behind the shuttlecock, transfer weight from back to front foot, extend your racket arm at the highest point, and snap your forearm and wrist downward with full pronation.

Pro Tip: Aim for steep angle rather than pure brute strength; a steep smash is much harder to retrieve than a flat fast one.

Gear Guide

Choosing the Right Equipment

A quick breakdown of rackets, shuttlecocks, and court essentials.

🏸

Rackets

  • Head-Heavy: Powerful smashes, slower defense.
  • Head-Light: Fast reflex defense, doubles play.
  • Even-Balance: Versatile all-around play.
  • Weights: 3U (85-89g), 4U (80-84g - most popular).
🪶

Shuttlecocks

  • Feather (Goose/Duck): Authentic flight trajectory, required for tournament competition.
  • Nylon / Plastic: Highly durable, cost-effective for casual games and training.
  • Speed Ratings: Rated 76-78 depending on temperature and altitude.
👟

Shoes & Grip

  • Non-Marking Sole: Gum-rubber sole required on indoor synthetic and wooden courts.
  • Lateral Support: Protects ankles from sudden lunge stops and direction switches.
  • Grip: Overgrip (tacky or towel grip) absorbs sweat and prevents racket slipping.
